The review lens

Microscopy first. Meaning attached.

Cellwear’s concept is strongest when an image works visually from across the room and becomes more meaningful when someone learns what it reveals.

CONTEXT

Tell us what is visible.

Name the cells, tissue, biological process, or cancer connection as precisely as possible.

METHOD

Explain how it was seen.

Include the microscopy method, stain, labels, scale, and processing when known.

INTEGRITY

Separate observation from interpretation.

Describe what the image can teach without presenting it as a personal diagnosis.
